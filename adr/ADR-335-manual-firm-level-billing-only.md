# ADR-335: Manual Firm-Level Billing as the Only Tenant Billing Model — PayFast Recurring Subscription Path Removed, Invoice Pay-Now Kept

**Status**: Accepted

**Context**:

Phase 57 (ADR-219..222) built tenant subscriptions: a `Subscription` state machine on global `subscriptions`, trial/grace expiry detection, read-only enforcement via `SubscriptionGuardFilter`, and a **platform-level** PayFast integration (`billing/payfast/*`, `SubscriptionItnController` on `/api/webhooks/subscription`, `SubscriptionItnService`, `SubscriptionPayment` on global `subscription_payments`, `POST /api/billing/{subscribe,cancel}`, `GET /api/billing/payments`) that charged tenants a monthly card fee. ADR-220 deliberately kept that separate from the **tenant-level** PayFast gateway of Phase 25 (`integration/payment/PayFastPaymentGateway`, ADR-098..100), which lets a *client* pay a fee note online. Phase 58 (ADR-223) added `billing_method` as a separate dimension — `BillingMethod` {PAYFAST, DEBIT_ORDER, PILOT, COMPLIMENTARY, MANUAL} (`PILOT` used by demo provisioning, ADR-224) — with `AdminBillingController` (`/internal/billing/{set-plan,extend-trial,activate}`) and `PlatformBillingController` as the admin writers. `BillingMethod.java:21-27` `isTrialAutoExpiring()` is `this == PAYFAST || this == MANUAL`; `BillingResponse.java:35-36` derives `subscribable`/`cancellable` flags from PAYFAST; `SubscriptionExpiryJob.java:85` filters trial expiry on `List.of(PAYFAST, MANUAL)`; `AdminBillingService.java:130-134` refuses to set PAYFAST without a token.

The design-partner GTM motion (`.claude/ideas/gtm-design-partner-plan-2026-07-11.md`) prices pilots at firm level, manually; the founder decision (`requirements/claude-code-prompt-phase86.md`): **remove PayFast recurring tenant billing; keep the "TRIAL / MANUAL / MANAGED" billing-method model (the requirements' loose wording — there is no such enum), subscription guard, expiry handlers and admin overrides — "that *is* the pilot pricing model: firm-level, manual."** The architecture doc (`architecture/phase86-subtraction-legal-only.md` §11.1, §11.3.7, §11.9) lists the surviving methods as the real enum minus PAYFAST: {DEBIT_ORDER, PILOT, COMPLIMENTARY, MANUAL}; the requirements omit `PILOT`. The doc wins — `BillingMethod.PILOT` survives because `DemoProvisionServiceTest` and `demo/*` depend on it — and this ADR records the four-value set. Scouting also corrected the requirements' blanket "remove PayFast": only the platform path goes (§11.3.10(d)).

**Options Considered**:

### A. The tenant billing model

1. **Delete the PayFast recurring subscription path; keep `BillingMethod` {DEBIT_ORDER, PILOT, COMPLIMENTARY, MANUAL} (PAYFAST removed) and the `SubscriptionStatus` lifecycle, `SubscriptionGuardFilter`, expiry handlers and admin overrides (CHOSEN)** — delete `billing/payfast/**`, the ITN controller/service, `SubscriptionPayment` + repository, `SubscribeResponse`/`PaymentResponse`, the three `BillingController` endpoints, `SubscriptionService.initiateSubscribe`/cancel/payment history, the five PayFast columns on `subscriptions`, `heykazi.billing.{return-url,cancel-url,notify-url,payfast.*}` in yml and compose env, the `/api/webhooks/subscription` `permitAll` in `SecurityConfig`, and the subscribe/cancel/payment-history frontend components; trim the PAYFAST-dependent semantics — `isTrialAutoExpiring()` becomes `this == MANUAL` (DEBIT_ORDER/PILOT/COMPLIMENTARY continue not to auto-expire trials, unchanged for them), `isAdminManaged()` (constant-true) deleted with its callers, `BillingResponse.subscribable`/`cancellable` removed (no self-serve path remains), `SubscriptionExpiryJob` filters on `List.of(MANUAL)`, `AdminBillingService` loses the PAYFAST branch; `SubscriptionLifecycleTest` pins the new truth table.
   - Pros: the only remaining writers of subscription state are `AdminBillingController` and `PlatformBillingController` — exactly the pilot operating model; `SubscriptionGuardFilter` semantics are untouched (TRIALING until `trial_ends_at`; every surviving method active or read-only after grace per `SubscriptionStatus`; `/api/billing/`, `/internal/`, `/actuator/`, `/portal/` exempt); one fewer `permitAll` webhook on the public surface; global `subscription_payments` and five columns are dropped by slice 8's own throwaway global migration (ADR-331) with no data to migrate.
   - Cons: adding card billing later is a rebuild, not an un-flag; `currency` on `subscriptions` may become unused once `monthly_amount_cents` goes (verify at build); `BillingProperties` keeps only trial/grace day settings.
2. **Keep PayFast dormant behind a flag** (e.g. `heykazi.billing.payfast.enabled=false`, or a `BillingMethod.PSP` value that no tenant uses).
   - Pros: re-enabling is configuration; ADR-219..222 stay literally true.
   - Cons: forbidden by "Delete, don't disable"; a dormant `permitAll` ITN webhook and `SubscriptionItnIntegrationTest`/`PlatformPayFastServiceTest` keep running in every verify; dead `PAYFAST_*` env rows persist in `compose/.env.prod.example` and `DEPLOY-VPS.md`; the subscribe CTA in the TRIAL banner would still have to be hidden, which is UI gating of a feature nobody can use.
3. **Delete the whole `billing` package**, including the guard; enforce pilot terms off-platform.
   - Pros: largest reduction.
   - Cons: `SubscriptionGuardFilter` is the only mechanism that makes an expired trial read-only; `demo/*`, `member/MemberSyncService`, `provisioning` and `security` consume `billing`; an expired pilot with full write access is a POPIA and commercial exposure no contract clause fixes.
4. **Replace PayFast with another PSP or Xero-driven invoicing of the firm.**
   - Pros: automated collection from firms.
   - Cons: a new feature in a subtraction phase; the pilot model is manual by decision, and with a handful of design partners there is nothing to automate yet.

### B. The tenant-level invoice pay-now gateway

1. **Keep `integration/payment` in full, including `PayFastPaymentGateway`, `payfast.sandbox`, `PaymentIntegrationCard`, `PaymentEventHistory`, `MockPaymentController` + `mock-payment.html` (CHOSEN)**.
   - Pros: online pay-now on a fee note is a legal feature on the keep list; it is driven by per-tenant `OrgIntegration` config (verify at build), not by the removed platform env; `invoice/{PaymentLinkService,PaymentReconciliationService,InvoiceTransitionService}` depend on it; the legal lifecycle's pay-now step re-verifies it.
   - Cons: "PayFast" still appears in the codebase, which the requirements' blanket wording did not anticipate — ADR-220's platform/tenant split is now the only reason the word survives.
2. **Remove all PayFast code, including pay-now.**
   - Pros: literal reading of "remove PayFast".
   - Cons: deletes a kept legal feature and breaks the lifecycle scenario; conflates two integrations ADR-220 separated on purpose.
3. **Keep only the Mock gateway** for e2e; drop the real PSP implementations.
   - Pros: test infrastructure stays green.
   - Cons: a pilot firm could never actually collect online; the gateway interface (ADR-098) would have no production implementation.

**Decision**: The manual billing-method model — `BillingMethod` {DEBIT_ORDER, PILOT, COMPLIMENTARY, MANUAL} with the PAYFAST method removed, and the unchanged `SubscriptionStatus` lifecycle (TRIALING → ACTIVE → … → LOCKED) — is the only tenant billing model (A1). (The requirements' "TRIAL / MANUAL / MANAGED" wording named arrangements loosely; TRIALING is a status, not a method — verified against `billing/BillingMethod.java` and `Subscription.java`.) The PayFast recurring subscription path — ITN webhook, checkout, cancel, payment history, `subscription_payments`, PayFast columns, env and frontend — is deleted in slice 8. The tenant invoice pay-now gateway (`integration/payment`) is explicitly kept (B1). Re-adding recurring billing later means a new `billing/psp` package behind a new `BillingMethod` value (alongside `MANUAL`/`PILOT`/`COMPLIMENTARY`/`DEBIT_ORDER`), with its own webhook permit and ITN handling, restored from the `horizontal-platform-final` tag as reference (ADR-330) — not an un-flag.

**Rationale**:

1. **The pilot pricing model is firm-level and manual by decision.** Design partners are invoiced and activated by hand through `AdminBillingController`; a self-serve card checkout contradicts that and would advertise a plan model the product does not have (see the no-plan-tier decision in project memory).
2. **Zero production tenants means no subscription or payment history to preserve.** `subscription_payments` and the five PayFast columns are dropped by slice 8's throwaway global migration and omitted by the squash (ADR-331) with nothing to migrate; this is free now and a data migration after Phase 84.
3. **Fewer public endpoints.** `/api/webhooks/subscription` is a `permitAll` route; removing it is one of only two `SecurityConfig` edits in the phase. `/api/webhooks/payment/**` (pay-now) stays because it serves a legal feature.
4. **Solo-operator continuity.** A PSP integration is a maintenance surface — sandbox credentials, ITN signature changes, reconciliation edge cases — with no revenue behind it. `SubscriptionGuardFilter`, the expiry job and three admin endpoints are the whole operating model a solo operator needs for a handful of firms.
5. **ADR-220's split is what makes the cut clean.** Because platform and tenant PayFast were built as separate services with separate config, slice 8 touches only `billing/`, two `SecurityConfig` lines, yml, compose env and `components/billing` — independent enough to run in a parallel worktree (§11.10).

**Consequences**:

- Positive: `billing/` keeps `BillingMethod` (four values; `isTrialAutoExpiring()` → `this == MANUAL`), `Subscription` (trimmed), `SubscriptionGuardFilter`, `SubscriptionExpiryJob` (trial-expiry filter `List.of(MANUAL)`), the trial/grace/cancellation handlers, `SubscriptionStatusCache`, `AdminBillingController`, `PlatformBillingController`, `AdminBillingService` (PAYFAST branch gone), `BillingResponse` (without `subscribable`/`cancellable`); `GET /api/billing/subscription` remains; `SubscriptionGuardWithoutPayFastTest` plus unchanged `SubscriptionGuardFilterTest` and a `SubscriptionLifecycleTest` extended with the `isTrialAutoExpiring` truth table (MANUAL true; DEBIT_ORDER/PILOT/COMPLIMENTARY false) prove the semantics.
- Positive: the schema change is slice 8's own throwaway global drop migration (`subscription_payments` + the five PayFast columns, ADR-331), so `Subscription` and `subscriptions` match from the slice's merge onward rather than only after the squash.
- Positive: `settings/billing` shows method and status only; `subscription-banner` keeps the TRIALING/expired banner without a "Subscribe now" CTA; `platform-admin/billing` keeps the tenant table and method override.
- Positive: the other `billing` consumers are untouched — `demo/{DemoCleanupService,DemoProvisionService}` (PILOT provisioning and cleanup, ADR-224/226), `member/MemberSyncService` (`BillingProperties` trial/grace days), `provisioning` and `security`; `SubscriptionGuardFilter`'s exemption list (`/api/billing/`, `/internal/`, `/actuator/`, `/portal/`) and its wiring in `SecurityConfig` do not change.
- Negative: no self-serve path from trial to paid — every activation is a platform-admin action; this does not scale past the design-partner cohort and is accepted as such.
- Negative: re-adding recurring billing is a rebuild (`billing/psp`, webhook permit, ITN verification, payment history) against a `Subscription` entity that no longer has PayFast columns; the tag is the only reference.
- Negative: ADR-219..222 and the PayFast parts of ADR-223..226 are marked Superseded by ADR-330; `BillingPropertiesTest` loses its PayFast cases; `BillingControllerIntegrationTest` shrinks to one endpoint; `compose/.env.prod.example` and `DEPLOY-VPS.md` lose `PAYFAST_*` rows.
- Negative: `Subscription.currency` may be orphaned once `monthly_amount_cents` goes; the slice-8 builder must verify and either drop it in the same PR (so the baseline never carries it) or document why it stays — a dangling column in `V1__baseline.sql` would be immutable after Phase 84 (ADR-331).
- Negative: the word "PayFast" survives in `integration/payment` and marketing copy (pay-now mention kept, subscription mention removed) and will need explaining to anyone reading the requirements' blanket wording.
