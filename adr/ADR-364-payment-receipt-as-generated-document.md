# ADR-364: Payment receipts are generated documents keyed to payment events

**Status**: Accepted

**Context**:

A paid fee note's only download, on the portal and in the firm app, is the PDF generated at send time: `InvoiceEmailEventListener.java:69-105` renders `fee-note-za` once in a REQUIRES_NEW transaction, and `PortalInvoiceController.java:115-121` → `PortalReadModelService.getInvoiceDownloadUrl` (`PortalReadModelService.java:167-181`; returns the presigned URL as a `String`, which the controller wraps in its nested `PortalDownloadResponse`) presigns the **most recent** `GeneratedDocument` with `primary_entity_type = INVOICE` for the invoice. Nothing exists that a client can keep as proof of payment after Sipho pays INV-0001 (G88-F32, MEDIUM; founder decision 6 builds it). The facts a receipt needs are all persisted: `payment_events` (`invoice/PaymentEvent.java`: `invoice_id, provider_slug, session_id, payment_reference, status (COMPLETED…), amount, currency, payment_destination, payment_method (nullable for pre-column rows), created_at`) written by the single posting path `InvoiceTransitionService.postPayment` (`:345-396`, [ADR-347](ADR-347-fee-note-due-date-and-balance-model.md)), which also publishes `InvoicePaidEvent` / `InvoicePartiallyPaidEvent` (both sealed `DomainEvent` records); `Invoice.amount_paid`, `paid_at` and the balance; and the firm letterhead ([ADR-353](ADR-353-firm-letterhead-settings.md)) that the fee note already renders through `org.*`. The rendering pipeline is generic: `GeneratedDocumentService.generateDocument(templateId, entityId, saveToDocuments, acknowledgeWarnings, clauseSelections, memberId, intendedVisibility, extraContext, extraFormatHints, notifyClient)` (`:195`) with `extraContext` as the hook for per-call data, `loopTable` for row lists (`TemplateVariableAnalyzer.java:105`), and identity-based keys since [ADR-359](ADR-359-identity-based-generated-document-keys.md).

Two traps shape the design. `InvoiceContextBuilder` carries no payment data (no `amountPaid`, `balanceDue`, `paidAt`, no payment list). And a receipt persisted as an INVOICE-typed `GeneratedDocument` would silently replace the fee-note download because `getInvoiceDownloadUrl` takes `docs.getFirst()` of all INVOICE documents ordered by `generated_at DESC`. `TemplateCategory` has no RECEIPT value (`ENGAGEMENT_LETTER, STATEMENT_OF_WORK, COVER_LETTER, PROJECT_SUMMARY, NDA, REPORT, COMPLIANCE, CLOSURE, OTHER`); `TemplateEntityType` is `PROJECT, CUSTOMER, INVOICE`.

**Options Considered**:

1. **A `receipts` table (tenant V10) with its own entity, numbering sequence, renderer call and download endpoints.**
   - Pros:
     - A receipt becomes a first-class record with a stored number and a direct FK to the payment event.
   - Cons:
     - Duplicates what `generated_documents` already stores (template, entity, file name, key, size, context snapshot, generated-at, generated-by) and what `payment_events` already is (one row per payment, amount, method, reference, date); the requirements said "no new table unless architecture proves one is needed" and nothing here needs one.
     - Numbering does not need an entity: a per-fee-note sequence is two columns (`invoices.receipt_sequence`, `payment_events.receipt_number`), not a table.
2. **Render on the fly on every download, never persist (receipt = a view over `payment_events`).**
   - Pros:
     - No column, no listener, nothing to get out of sync.
   - Cons:
     - A receipt is evidence; if the firm changes its letterhead, VAT number or the template, a client re-downloading last year's receipt must get the document they were issued, not a re-rendering — exactly why fee notes are generated once at send. `context_snapshot` on `generated_documents` is the audit of what was printed.
     - Every portal click costs a Tiptap → PDF render; the fee note's download path is a presign.
3. **A pack template `payment-receipt-za` rendered through the generic generation pipeline, one `GeneratedDocument` per COMPLETED payment event, linked by a nullable FK column on `payment_events`, generated AFTER_COMMIT with a lazy fallback on first download (CHOSEN).**
   - Pros:
     - Reuses the letterhead, the reconcile ([ADR-356](ADR-356-pack-content-reconciliation.md)), the identity-based key, the context snapshot, the existing firm download (`/api/generated-documents/{id}/download`) and the `extraContext` seam; the only schema change is one nullable column.
     - Generation on the payment event mirrors the fee note's send-time generation (same listener shape, same REQUIRES_NEW, same "never fail the business transaction" rule), and the lazy fallback covers listener failure and pre-existing payments without a backfill.
     - The payment-event id in the event `details` ties the receipt to exactly one payment, so partial payments get one receipt each with the balance after that payment.
   - Cons:
     - The fee-note download must be filtered to the fee-note template or the receipt hijacks it; this is a required change to `getInvoiceDownloadUrl`, with an integration test.
     - The receipt number must be **stored** (`{invoiceNumber}/R{seq}` from `invoices.receipt_sequence`, written to `payment_events.receipt_number` under the invoice row lock): a reversal deletes the payment event (`InvoiceTransitionService.java:646`), so a derived ordinal would renumber every later receipt; the reversal must also delete the receipt document.
4. **Extend the fee-note PDF: regenerate `fee-note-za` with a "Payments received" block after each payment and let the one download serve as the receipt.**
   - Pros:
     - No new template, no new download.
   - Cons:
     - Overwrites the document that was sent (the fee note is the tax invoice; its content must not change after issue), and the partial-payment case needs a per-payment document anyway; it also re-triggers the "latest INVOICE document" trap on every payment.

**Decision**: Option 3. **No new table.** Tenant V10 §1 (next free at build time): `payment_events.receipt_generated_document_id uuid REFERENCES generated_documents(id) ON DELETE SET NULL`, `payment_events.receipt_number varchar(40) NULL` and `invoices.receipt_sequence int NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`. **Template** `payment-receipt-za` in the legal-za pack (`primaryEntityType INVOICE`, category **OTHER** — no new `TemplateCategory` value), resolved by key like the fee note (`findPackSeeded("legal-za", "payment-receipt-za")`), letterhead block as the fee note's. **Context**: `InvoiceContextBuilder` gains `invoice.amountPaid`, `invoice.balanceDue`, `invoice.paidAt`, `invoice.payments[]` (date, method label, amount, reference, balance after); the receipt's own payment goes in `extraContext` as `payment.{id, number, date, methodLabel, amount, reference, balanceAfter}`; receipt number `{invoiceNumber}/R{seq}` — `PaymentReceiptService` increments `invoices.receipt_sequence` under the invoice row lock (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`) and stores the number on the payment event before rendering; numbers are monotonic and never reused. **Reversal** (`InvoiceTransitionService.java:646` deletes the payment event) also deletes the receipt `GeneratedDocument` — a void receipt must not stay downloadable — and the reversal audit row records the receipt number. **Trigger**: `@TransactionalEventListener(AFTER_COMMIT)` on `InvoicePaidEvent` / `InvoicePartiallyPaidEvent` (both gain an additive `payment_event_id` in `details`: `InvoiceTransitionService.java:394-396` currently discards the return of `paymentEventRepository.save(...)`, so the publisher captures `var saved = …`; the listener resolves the matter from the invoice lines because `InvoicePaidEvent` publishes `projectId = null` at `:459`), REQUIRES_NEW, `saveToDocuments=false`, actor = `event.actorMemberId()` when a member posted the payment, else `invoice.createdBy` (provider-posted and portal-lazy generations have no acting member and `generated_documents.generated_by` is `NOT NULL`; a per-tenant system member is an open question in §14.12); failure logged, never fails the payment. **Lazy fallback**: the download endpoints lock the payment-event row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), re-read the FK and return the existing document, generating only when it is null; `attachReceipt` is idempotent, so a listener and a lazy call cannot conflict. **Portal**: `GET /portal/invoices/{id}/payments` → `[{paymentEventId, date, methodLabel, amount, reference, receiptAvailable}]`; `GET /portal/invoices/{id}/payments/{paymentEventId}/receipt` → `PortalDownloadResponse`; "Download receipt" per payment row on PAID and PARTIALLY_PAID detail pages. **Firm**: `PaymentEventResponse` gains `receiptGeneratedDocumentId` and `receiptNumber`; download via the existing generated-document endpoint; the invoice page's payments section shows the link, and a "Generate receipt" button calls `POST /api/invoices/{id}/payment-events/{paymentEventId}/receipt` (`INVOICING`, the lazy path) when the FK is null. **Fee-note download fix**: `getInvoiceDownloadUrl` filters to the resolved fee-note template id — never "latest INVOICE document".

**Rationale**:

1. **The receipt's identity is the payment event.** [ADR-347](ADR-347-fee-note-due-date-and-balance-model.md) made every payment source post its actual amount through one path into `payment_events`; a receipt that is anything other than "the document for that row" would invent a second ledger. The FK lives on the payment event because that is the row the receipt is about; `generated_documents` keeps pointing at the invoice as its primary entity because the renderer and the download endpoints are invoice-typed.
2. **No new enum value — the cross-layer census lesson.** A `RECEIPT` category would touch `TemplateCategory`, the template-settings filter UI, the terminology census ([ADR-357](ADR-357-terminology-guard-extension.md)), the pack validator and every category label map, for a template that is only ever resolved by key, exactly like `fee-note-za` and `trust-receipt` (both already `OTHER`). Stored literals do not change ([ADR-342](ADR-342-audit-and-entity-tag-strings-stable-under-renamed-wire.md)); the rulings §3 rule "no new enum values" applies.
3. **Generated once, kept, like the fee note.** The receipt is client-facing evidence; `context_snapshot` records what was printed, the identity-based key ([ADR-359](ADR-359-identity-based-generated-document-keys.md)) guarantees two receipts never share bytes, and the letterhead comes from [ADR-353](ADR-353-firm-letterhead-settings.md) at generation time. AFTER_COMMIT + REQUIRES_NEW is the listener shape every projection uses; the lazy fallback is what makes "a receipt is always downloadable" true without a backfill runner for payments posted before this phase.
4. **The hijack trap is fixed at its root, with a test on the real path.** Filtering `getInvoiceDownloadUrl` to the fee-note template id is the only correct reading of "the fee note's download" once more than one INVOICE-typed document exists per invoice; the integration test pays an invoice (which generates a receipt) and then downloads the fee note, asserting the bytes are the fee note's. The portal walk observes Sipho downloading a receipt for INV-0001 after paying, and the PDF is observed in a real browser at 1024 and 1440 (Phase 88: PDF output needs a real observer).
5. **Schema-per-tenant and the portal read model are untouched.** The column and the template are tenant-side; the portal reads through `PortalReadModelService` with the same ownership check the fee-note download uses; no global table changes.

**Consequences**:

- Positive:
  - A PAID or PARTIALLY_PAID fee note offers one receipt per payment on both surfaces; partial payments each carry their balance-after.
  - Tenant V10 §1 gains two columns on `payment_events` and one on `invoices`; the legal-za pack gains one template at **v12** (after 632A's v11; next free at build time — new template rows are created on existing tenants by the installer's "create missing").
  - The firm app can generate a missing receipt explicitly (`POST …/payment-events/{id}/receipt` + a "Generate receipt" button) — the same lazy path the portal download uses.
  - The fee-note download becomes deterministic by template, closing a latent defect for any future INVOICE-typed document (e.g. a credit note).
- Negative:
  - `InvoiceContextBuilder` grows four keys and a list; `fee-note-za` ignores them, but the context snapshot of every future fee note carries them.
  - Method labels for `payment_method = NULL` (pre-column events) must render as "Payment", not blank or "null"; the label map is a frontend/portal concern and must be observed.
  - Provider-posted payments (PSP webhook) have no member actor; the receipt's `generated_by` is `invoice.createdBy` (the fee-note creator, which both events already carry as `createdByMemberId`), and the audit entry says so; a per-tenant system member is the §14.12 alternative.
  - Receipt numbering is stored and monotonic: a reversal deletes the payment event and its receipt document but never decrements `receipt_sequence`, so a client who kept `INV-0001/R2` can never receive a different document under that number; the reversal audit row carries the number.
- Follow-ups / not done: no receipt e-mail (client e-mails are explicit-intent only, [ADR-350](ADR-350-client-emails-explicit-intent.md)); no receipt for trust-transfer settlements beyond the payment event they already post; no `RECEIPT` category; no backfill of receipts for historical payments (lazy generation covers them on first download).
