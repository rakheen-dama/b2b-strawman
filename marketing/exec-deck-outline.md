# Kazi — Exec Presentation (Pilot-Firm Partners)

> Audience: the managing partner (and possibly the bookkeeper/practice manager) of a 2–15 attorney SA firm. Goal: leave the meeting with a signed pilot letter, or a named objection. 10 slides, ~20 minutes talk + demo, rest conversation. Rendered deck: `decks/exec-deck.html`.
>
> Structure follows the GTM pitch spine: pain → what Kazi is → trust credibility → AI moment (live demo) → take-on → continuity → pilot offer → ask.

---

**Slide 1 — Title.**
*Kazi — Run your firm without the month-end dread.* Practice management for South African law firms. kazi.africa.
Speaker note: One sentence of who you are and why this exists. Do not open with technology.

**Slide 2 — The three worst days of your month.**
Fee-note run (reconstructing the month from timesheets and memory) · Trust reconciliation (Excel + bank statement + hope; an LPA problem, not an admin problem) · Debtors & FICA admin (unpaid fee notes nobody chases; FICA files that live in a cabinet).
Speaker note: Pause here. Ask "which of these is worst for you?" — their answer re-orders the rest of the meeting. Capture it verbatim.

**Slide 3 — What Kazi is.**
One system for the firm's back office: **Matters & time** (everything captured as it happens) → **Fee notes** (drafted from recorded time, house style, VAT, contingency caps) → **Trust accounting** (LPA s86) → **FICA** (per-client checklist, not a cabinet) → **Debtors** (statements & follow-up on a schedule) → **Client portal** (clients see status & documents; fewer "any update?" calls).
Speaker note: This is the only breadth slide. Six pillars, one line each, move on. Resist the tour.

**Slide 4 — Trust accounting you can defend.**
Every trust payment approval-gated · Immutable audit trail on every entry · Continuous three-way reconciliation (bank ↔ cashbook ↔ client ledgers) · Auditor-ready exports.
Speaker note: The credibility beat. If an auditor relationship exists, name-drop the review process here ("we're putting this in front of trust auditors before GA").

**Slide 5 — The part nobody else has.** *(live demo slide)*
Point **your own AI** at your practice: draft the month's fee notes from unbilled time · screen the trust account against s86 · check a client's FICA gaps and draft the request · file an email into the right matter.
Speaker note: Stop presenting, start demoing — 5 minutes, demo data. The moment to land: Claude drafts, **a human approves inside Kazi**. Close the demo with: "your data, your AI, your approval."

**Slide 6 — Switching is our job, not yours.**
You provide exports (GhostPractice / AJS / Lexpro / bookkeeper spreadsheets — CSV is fine). We migrate clients, open matters, debtor balances, trust balances. Trust take-on is auditor-defensible: nothing commits until client balances tie to your bank balance at cutover, to the cent. You get a take-on report.
Speaker note: This kills the #1 unspoken blocker. "I do this personally for pilot firms" — say it.

**Slide 7 — Your data is always yours.**
Full export any time, by you (built, not promised) · Documented take-off path — leave with everything, usable formats · Exit clause written into the pilot agreement.
Speaker note: Pre-empt the bus question before they ask it; it builds more trust volunteered than extracted. If asked about hosting/POPIA: POPIA-conscious by design, data egress to AI is consent-gated per firm.

**Slide 8 — The design-partner pilot.**
Free for 6 months · Founder-run take-on and onboarding · Runs in parallel with your current system — no burned bridges · Full export + exit clause if you walk.
Speaker note: Emphasize scarcity honestly: 2–3 firms only, because each gets white-glove treatment.

**Slide 9 — What we ask in return.**
30 minutes of feedback per week · Permission to use the firm as a reference at launch · After the pilot: flat firm-level fee, ~R2,500–R5,000/month by firm size, unlimited users — no per-seat pricing.
Speaker note: Price transparency now avoids a sour month-7 conversation. Anchor the range, don't negotiate in the room.

**Slide 10 — Next step.**
One hour with your bookkeeper/practice manager + sample exports → we confirm the migration plan → pilot letter → you're live within [n] weeks. hello@kazi.africa · kazi.africa/legal.
Speaker note: The close is a calendar invite, not a signature. Book the bookkeeper session before leaving the room.
