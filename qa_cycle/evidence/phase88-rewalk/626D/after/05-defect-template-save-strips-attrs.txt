Mechanism: React server-action reply encoding serialises non-plain (null-prototype) objects as temporary
references "$T"; on the server they are opaque and JSON.stringify in lib/api/client.ts drops them.
TemplateEditorClient.handleSave passes editor.getJSON() straight to updateTemplateAction(...).
Fix direction (not applied, out of scope): pass a plain clone, e.g. JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(editorContent)) or structuredClone→plain.
Frontend/backend files involved are untouched by PR #1688 (backend-only, pack content + seeder/renderer).
