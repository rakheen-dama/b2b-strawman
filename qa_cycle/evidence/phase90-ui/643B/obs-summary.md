## Defects
- **D1 (MEDIUM, layout): the Matter Profitability ranking chart's category labels overlap each other.**
  - **Cause:** `BarChartPreset` horizontal (`components/charts/bar-chart.tsx:306-313`) draws a `YAxis` with `width={120}`, and recharts word-wraps longer tick text inside that width. The bar band is fixed at `HORIZONTAL_BAR_BAND_PX = 30` (`components/charts/layout.ts`), so any matter name that wraps to 3 or more lines (about 36 px or more) spills into the neighbouring bands.
  - **Observed** at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440, in light and dark. 5 of 6 adjacent label pairs overlap, by 3 px or 15 px. "Ndlovu v RAF — Depth II …" prints on top of "Apex Shareholder Agreement …", and "Apex" itself is completely covered. "QC Debt Recovery #1" also collides.
  - **Not caused by the e2e suffixes:** the realistic name "Apex Shareholder Agreement" alone wraps to 3 lines (Apex / Shareholder / Agreement) at 120 px.
  - **Impact:** the chart's purpose is to identify which matters rank where, and the labels are illegible. The "View as table" panel and the matter table below still carry the full names.
  - **Scope:** the kit code dates from 639B/640B, but 643B is the first consumer with long category names (matters). `components/profitability/profitability-ranking-chart.tsx` passes nothing that limits label length, and the utilization chart carries the same risk with long fee-earner names.
  - **Evidence:** `after/matter-ranking-chart-1440-light.png`, `after/matter-ranking-chart-1024-dark.png`, after.json `matterRanking.tickOverlapsPx` (per-viewport overlap pairs in px).
  - **Repro:** as Alice, open `/org/e2e-test-org/reports/profitability` after the legal-lifecycle suite, at any width.

### New finding
- **D2 (LOW, kit layout, residual of the D1 fix): all-caps or wide-glyph category names can still be clipped at the left of the chart.**
  - **Cause:** the budget `CATEGORY_TICK_MAX_CHARS = floor((120 − 6 − 8) / 7) = 15` assumes 7 px per character. Uppercase glyphs at 12 px are wider, so a 15-character label can exceed the 106 px label column, and the chart `svg` has `overflow: hidden`.
  - **Probe:** this was a DOM-only, non-persistent check. A rendered ranking tick was cloned with the truncated sample text, and the same font and position were measured. No data was created.

    | Name | Width | Clipped |
    |---|---|---|
    | "STANDARD BANK v NKOSI" → "STANDARD BANK…" | 110 px | 4 px |
    | "WOOLWORTHS HOL…" | 126 px | about 20 px |
    | "ABSA BANK LIMI…" | 104.6 px | none (fits) |
    | "Mokoena Holdin…" | 100.8 px | none |
    | pathological "MMMMMMMMMMMMMM…" | 156 px | 50 px past the svg, 25 px past the card |

    The screenshot shows "STANDARD BANK…" losing part of its first letter and "WOOLWORTHS HOL…" rendering as "OLWORTHS HOL…".
  - **Impact:** a client or matter name typed in capitals, which is common for company names, loses its first letters in the ranking, utilization or WIP chart. The `<title>`, tooltip, summary and table still carry the full name.
  - **Not observed with the seeded data:** every real tick fits.
  - **Possible remedies (not applied):** a smaller character budget, or measuring the text and truncating by width.
  - **Evidence:** `after-fix/probe-allcaps-tick-clip-1440-light.png`, after-fix.json `allCapsTickProbe`.
- **Note N5:** at 15 characters, names that share a long prefix become indistinguishable on the axis. "Ndlovu v RAF m…" and "Ndlovu v RAF —…" are still distinct here, but "Ndlovu v RAF — Depth II" and "… Depth III" would not be. This is inherent to fixed truncation. The `<title>`, tooltip and table disambiguate.
