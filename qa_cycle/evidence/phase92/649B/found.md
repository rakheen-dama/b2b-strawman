# 649B — out-of-scope findings (reported, not fixed; re-spec into the named slice)

Found while building 649B (contra reversal model). Each is a reader that filters on `status` / `transaction_type`
instead of summing posted effects — ADR-378: "any reader that filtered on status instead of summing effects would be
wrong". Under the contra model a reversed original stays posted as `REVERSED` and its `REVERSAL` row carries the
negation; these readers see only half of the pair. None changes a derived balance (all of `TrustLedgerQueryRepository`
sums `posted_at IS NOT NULL` rows); severity is lower than D15 (fixed in this slice, `findForStatement`).

| # | Reader (main code) | Under the contra model | Suggested slice |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | `TrustTransactionRepository.findForReceiptsPayments` (`status IN ('RECORDED','APPROVED') AND transactionType <> 'REVERSAL'`) → Trust Receipts & Payments report | omits both halves of a reversed pair (the `REVERSED` original and its contra). Internally consistent (net 0), but the report no longer lists a payment the bank did make and the return the bank did make. **CodeRabbit (PR #1789, `TrustTransactionRepository` ~:284-285) example:** a March payment reversed in April vanishes from the March report and the April contra is excluded from April — the pair nets to zero across both months, but neither month reports its posted cashbook movements; each movement must be reported in the period of its own posting date | 646C (books/reports over posted effects) |
| F2 | `TrustTransactionRepository.findClientHistoryWithRunningBalance` (client-ledger `/history` window: `status IN ('RECORDED','APPROVED') AND transaction_type <> 'REVERSAL'`) | `REVERSED` originals and `REVERSAL` rows carry `runningBalance = null` (pinned by `ClientLedgerRunningBalanceIntegrationTest`), and intermediate running balances between the original's and the contra's dates omit the original. The first posted row still equals `clientBalance(today)`. **CodeRabbit (PR #1789, `TrustTransactionRepository` ~:100-101) example:** deposit 100, payment 40, deposit 10, the payment reversed on a later date — the third row must show 70 as at its date; the window drops the original payment (and its contra), so it shows 110.
