## Deferred from the later CodeRabbit round on 82cd19bec (one-round rule; PR #1806 comment 6992902813)

- **F-12 (LOW, stability → 650D)** — `TrustPostingService.performApprovalCompletion`, UNIDENT_ALLOC_OUT: `postOnApproval` takes the account FOR SHARE lock before `onResolutionApproved` locks the destination client's anchor. A concurrent s86(4) `placeInvestment` for that client (anchor → account FOR UPDATE, per F-11) can deadlock with it, and one request fails with 40P01. Introduced by the C1 account lock. Fix: lock the item and destination anchor before the first posting lock, in the F-10/F-11 global order, with a `pg_blocking_pids` test.
- **F-13 (design call → orchestrator/founder)** — the after-lock boundary `posted_at > locked_at` compares `clock.instant()` values taken on different hosts. The account lock orders the commits but not the timestamps. On a multi-host deployment with a lagging clock, a posting committed after the lock could fall outside the after-lock schedule, and `BACKDATED_AFTER_LOCK` would not fire for it. The ledger itself stays correct. Options are a DB-side ordering (`clock_timestamp()` / `transaction_timestamp()` for both stamps, or a sequence or xid watermark stored on the list). Current deployments are single-host.
- **F-14 (→ 650C)** — when the year end is a locked month end, the Section 35 pack shows locked client balances without the after-lock explanation (with F-4 / O-OBS-3).
