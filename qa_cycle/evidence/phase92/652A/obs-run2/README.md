## Notes

1. **What r2 success can and cannot show in a browser.** Next.js sends server actions one at a time. While the calculate was held, S2's upload did not leave the browser (`uploadedDuringHold: false`). Its POST (`603dc338…`) went out at 12:00:34.884, 57 ms after the release. So the held result always lands before the upload's own reset. A missing guard on `setReconciliation` would be hidden by that reset, and the browser cannot tell the difference there. Vitest pins that write. The guard that *is* visible in the browser is the banner and stale flag. r2 refused shows it: after the reset, a released refusal produced no banner.
2. **Calculate response bodies.** Chromium kept no body for most of the routed action responses ("No data found for resource"). The outcomes come from the audit trail (`trust_reconciliation.calculated` is written on each successful calculate only) and from `updated_at`. The r1-b body was captured verbatim.
3. **`requestfailed net::ERR_ABORTED`** shows up in `actions` for actions that did succeed (manualMatch, exclude, create …; the DB confirms each). Chromium reports this when the client closes a streamed RSC response after reading it. The only real failures are the four `net::ERR_FAILED` console errors, from the four deliberate aborts.
4. **Residue on the stack.** Two DRAFT reconciliations (R1 `9136e7fc…`, R2 `7b8337b4…`), statements s1/s2/s3-HZV1Q, two deposits, two approved payments (5 and 9 Nov). R1's deeds line is still matched to `PAY/R1/HZV1Q` (from the r2-success match). Run 1's items show in the right pane, which is harmless.
5. **Defects found:** none.
