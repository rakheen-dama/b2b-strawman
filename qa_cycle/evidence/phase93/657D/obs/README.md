- **H**: new General account, not primary, opened 2026-01-01, no. 6575001. One client balance, B/B1 R50 000,00, at **30 September 2026**. Used for checks 6b and 6c.

**Adapted first month.** No UI posts a client deposit or payment to a non-primary account (Transactions → Record is primary-bound, F-657C-8). The R438 727,40 world with client movements is covered by `FirstPostCutoverReconciliationIntegrationTest`, not by this browser run.
