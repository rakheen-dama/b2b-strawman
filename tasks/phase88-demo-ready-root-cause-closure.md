# Phase 88 — Demo-Ready: Root-Cause Closure of the Phase 87 Capstone Gaps

Phase 88 is a **root-cause closure phase**: no new page family, no new navigation zone, no new capability, no change to the `matter`/`client` wire vocabulary or the pinned audit literals ([ADR-342](../adr/ADR-342-audit-and-entity-tag-strings-stable-under-renamed-wire.md)). The Phase 87 capstone (617C, PR #1641) walked the legal-za 90-day story on the real Keycloak stack and found 59 gaps (5 HIGH), 53 failed steps and 5 of 11 exit checkpoints failing; ten research dossiers (`tasks/phase88-research/R1…R10`) traced them to **24 root-cause units** ([register §2](phase88-gap-register.md)). Phase 88 fixes those units at the source in the register's order, with one tenant migration series starting at **V4** (V5+ once V4 has been applied to a persistent stack, §13.7) and one global migration (**V2**), re-walks the scenario twice — mid-phase (Days 1–7, 36–47, 61–75) and at the end (all 90 days) — and exits only on **observed** evidence: zero HIGH, zero FAIL outside the authorised scenario amendments (register §5), E.1/E.2/E.3/E.8/E.9 PASS, legal + default + portal Playwright green on a fresh stack. Architecture: [`architecture/phase88-demo-ready-root-cause-closure.md`](../architecture/phase88-demo-ready-root-cause-closure.md) (Section 13); requirements: [`requirements/claude-code-prompt-phase88.md`](../requirements/claude-code-prompt-phase88.md) (U1–U24); ADRs [345](../adr/ADR-345-canonical-project-role-values.md)–[357](../adr/ADR-357-terminology-guard-extension.md).
